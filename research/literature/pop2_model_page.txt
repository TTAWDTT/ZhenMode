Parallel Ocean Program (POP)
Main content
The ocean component of the CESM1.0 is the Parallel Ocean Program version 2 (POP2). This model is based on the POP version 2.1 of the Los Alamos National Laboratory; however, it includes many physical and software developments incorporated by the members of the Ocean Model Working Group (see the
notable improvements
page for these developments).
