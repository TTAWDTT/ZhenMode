# 365d Stage-F band-ice + fixed-MLD + cooling-gate v2 
 
Date: 2026-09-27 
 
Purpose: test whether the fixed 100m 40--60N mixed layer can be retained only in cooling season, preventing the late-season upper-ocean warming that rejected the ungated v2 run. 
 
Result: 365d run completed and passed stability, but the annual climate gate is rejected. Compared with the no-ice control, global A2 worsens from 1.204 to 1.315 C, NA raw RMSE from 0.975 to 1.947 C, global 3D RMSE from 1.547 to 1.574 C, and NA 3D RMSE from 1.276 to 1.878 C. Near-wall signed bias improves from -0.857 to +0.667 C, but the larger climate/3D failures dominate. Keep this as a diagnostic only; do not promote.
